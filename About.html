A Little About Me ♡

♡ Who I Am

I am seventeen years old and I live in the UK in a small town in Cheshire.

♡ My Personality

People need to stop mistaking me for being shy and quiet!!!! Once you get me talking about something I'm passionate about you don't hear the end of it. I'm definitely a 'yapper.' And I have a HUGE heart, I will forever advocate for the importance of wellbeing and I believe that we all succeed when we build each other up.

♡ My Interests

I LOVE music. It's likely you'll find me with headphones in listening to music in my own bubble 99.9% of the time. I also love all kinds of art and I tend to look for the deeper meanings behind what is expressed.

♡ My Goals

I dream of a career in technology, specifically cybersecurity. I particularly enjoy the ever-changing nature of developing cyber threats - I know cybersecurity is best suited to me as I'll admit I'm not a huge fan of repetition. I just hope that in the future I can make some big contributions to the industry and how we look at protecting organizations!
